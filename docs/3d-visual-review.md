# 3D Architectural Miniature Review

The original renderer preserved graph connections clearly but read primarily as a network of repeated towers. The approved upgrade therefore uses a **tabletop architectural miniature** rather than photorealistic mapping, preserving the clarity required for teaching graph algorithms and interventions.

| Design decision | Implemented treatment | Academic readability preserved by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facility differentiation | Pitched homes, low school and retail buildings, cross-shaped hospitals, and taller glazed offices | A facility’s form reinforces its role without replacing epidemic-state color |
| Roads and actions | Surface roads, primary-road markings, and bright road-closure barriers | Streets remain the visible graph corridors and closures remain unambiguous |
| City context | Parcels, civic green buffers, district lights, and a layered tabletop base | Context stays quiet beneath buildings and network signals |
| Epidemic and interventions | Animated infected rings, selected/seed halos, and perimeter quarantine fencing | Colors and physical markers communicate the scenario state at a glance |
| Camera | Tightened isometric default framing with bounded orbit controls | The full network remains visible while the city reads as the focal instrument |

The visual approach deliberately avoids photographic imagery, building textures, and opaque effects that could hide edge connectivity, containment barriers, or location state.
